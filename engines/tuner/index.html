The Tuner

Find out whose words your customer is repeating.

The situation

You replay a sales call with a security customer. The customer says 8 lines. Some lines are the customer's own words. Some lines repeat a competitor's pitch. One competitor is already in this account.

Your goal

For each line, answer two questions. Whose words are these: the customer's own, or a competitor's pitch? Which reply answers that source? At the end, you see which competitor is in the account.

What to do

  1. Read the customer's line on the white paper strip.
  2. Click a name under Source notes. Read what that source wants the customer to believe.
  3. Tune the dial to the source. Click Next source or Previous source, or drag the big knob. Each line starts on no source.
  4. Click a reply in the Your reply list.
  5. Click Submit answer.
  6. Read the result. Then click Next line.
